## Overview

This repository contains my undergraduate final-project report: a literature review of research on autonomous vehicles (AVs) and their potential effects on urban transportation systems.

The report examines **five published research papers**, summarizing their research questions, modeling assumptions, methodologies, and findings. It also discusses strengths, limitations, and possible directions for further investigation. Particular attention is given to the interaction between autonomous and human-driven vehicles, traffic flow, parking decisions, shared mobility, and travel demand.

**[Read the full report (PDF, in Persian)](./Final%20Report.pdf)**

## Topics Covered

- **Traffic-flow modeling:** Macroscopic, microscopic, and mesoscopic approaches to representing traffic behavior.
- **Cellular automata:** Modeling mixed autonomous and human-driven traffic using discrete vehicle-level rules.
- **Traffic assignment and parking:** Reviewing network-equilibrium models and variational-inequality formulations for autonomous vehicle route and parking choices.
- **Agent-based transportation simulation:** Examining shared autonomous vehicle fleets, dynamic ride-sharing, fleet sizing, and the MATSim framework.
- **Urban travel demand:** Reviewing the effects of self-parking and autonomous vehicle adoption on congestion, trip generation, and mode choice.

## Papers Reviewed

1. **Chen et al. (2020)** — *A future intelligent traffic system with mixed autonomous vehicles and human-driven vehicles*. Mixed-traffic behavior and cellular automata.
2. **Zhang et al. (2019)** — *A network traffic assignment model for autonomous vehicles with parking choices*. Traffic equilibrium and parking decisions.
3. **Fagnant & Kockelman (2016)** — *Dynamic ride-sharing and fleet sizing for a system of shared autonomous vehicles in Austin, Texas*. Agent-based shared-mobility simulation.
4. **Shafiei et al. (2021)** — *Impact of self-parking autonomous vehicles on urban traffic congestion*. Parking behavior and urban congestion.
5. **Truong et al. (2017)** — *Estimating the trip generation impacts of autonomous vehicles on car travel in Victoria, Australia*. Potential changes in travel demand.

Full bibliographic details and additional references appear in the report.

## Report Structure

The report introduces traffic-flow modeling approaches, then reviews each selected paper's methods and reported results, with commentary on their implications and limitations. It concludes with suggestions for further reading and a reference list.

## Scope

This is an **academic literature-review project**, not an original simulation implementation, experimental study, or peer-reviewed publication. Mathematical models, datasets, and numerical results discussed in the report originate from the cited research papers unless explicitly stated otherwise. The goal was to understand and compare approaches used to study autonomous vehicles in transportation systems.
